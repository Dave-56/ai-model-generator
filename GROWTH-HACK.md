# Growth Hack — Distribution Strategy

## The Core Constraint

Selling to **Scaler brand owners** — solo founders or tiny teams, time-poor, skeptical of AI, and discovery-constrained. They don't read tech blogs. They hang out in Facebook Groups, TikTok comment sections, and Shopify communities. Standard SaaS playbooks mostly don't apply.

Distribution has to meet them where they are. The **output is inherently visual** — which is both the biggest distribution asset and the thing most growth strategies for B2B SaaS ignore.

---

## The 5 Channels — Ranked by Leverage

### 1. The Output Is the Ad (Product-Led Virality)
**Highest-leverage, zero-cost channel.**

Every image generated gets uploaded to a Shopify store, posted to Instagram, or sent to a buyer. That image is a distribution event. Most companies building in this space completely ignore it.

**Tactics:**
- [ ] Free tier with subtle watermark / "Powered by [brand]" badge on download — small brands on free plans are fine with this
- [ ] **"How we shot our collection" shareable card** — when a brand gets their 30 shots, give them a one-click shareable: *"30 studio shots. 1 model. $0 photographer. 60 seconds."* Built to be screenshot-posted to Instagram Stories
- [ ] **Before/after export** — one-click side-by-side of flat lay + finished model shot. Inherently viral in the fashion brand community, zero effort from the merchant

No CAC. Pure compounding. Stop leaving it on the table.

---

### 2. Competitive Displacement — Steal Botika's Unhappy Customers
**Lowest CAC possible. They're already converted on the concept.**

Botika has 3,000 brands with documented, public complaints: 95% error rate, anatomy issues, multi-day wait times. SellerPic has billing fraud complaints. These are warm leads who already pay for this category, are already looking for an alternative, and have zero switching cost.

**Tactics:**
- [ ] Targeted Meta ads to audiences who follow or engage with Botika/AI fashion photography content — "Tired of getting unusable AI model photos?"
- [ ] **"Botika vs. [us]" comparison page** with side-by-side output quality — factual, not aggressive. High-intent search: "Botika alternative"
- [ ] Monitor 1-star Shopify App Store reviews on competitors — personal DM/email from founder ("Saw your review — we built something different, want to try it free?") converts extremely well at early stage
- [ ] **Switchover offer**: import their flat lays, generate their first collection free. The result closes the sale.

---

### 3. Print-on-Demand Platform Partnership
**One partnership = distribution to potentially hundreds of thousands of exact ICP.**

Printful and Printify serve millions of small brand owners who design products but have zero photography budget. They're exactly the Scaler. They have product images (mockups) but desperately want on-model shots.

This is a **platform embed** play, not just a referral:
- [ ] Partner with Printful/Printify to offer the tool natively in their workflow — "Generate on-model photos of your products" as a step after creating a product
- [ ] Revenue share model: they refer, we pay 20–30% of first-year subscription. Their incentive: value-add for merchants, platform stickiness, revenue for doing nothing
- [ ] Same logic applies to **Faire** (wholesale marketplace) — small brands listing there need professional photos to compete against bigger brands

> These take months to close. Start the conversation now.

---

### 4. The 60-Second Demo Flywheel (TikTok / YouTube Shorts)
**Where Scaler brand owners discover and share tools.**

Flat lay in, 3 studio shots in 60 seconds — one of the most demonstrable before/afters possible. Most SaaS companies have boring screencasts. This has a genuinely dramatic visual transformation.

**Tactics:**
- [ ] **Founder-led TikTok content**: raw demo, "I saved a brand owner $15,000 today" framing. The e-commerce TikTok community (small brand owners building in public) shares this aggressively
- [ ] **Seed 5–10 merchants** to post "I tried AI model photos for my brand" — they keep the images for free, their audience is our audience
- [ ] **YouTube SEO**: "how to get on-model photos without hiring a model" and "product photography for small fashion brands" — high-intent searches with weak existing content. A 5-minute tutorial ranks and converts for years

Goal: not viral, but a steady 3–5 new sign-ups per video. At 2 videos/week, that compounds.

---

### 5. The Shopify Agency Channel
**10 agencies × 50 clients = 500 customers. Repeatable and defensible.**

Shopify agencies and freelancers set up and manage stores for exactly the target brand. They constantly need to tell clients how to get product photos. Right now they say "hire a photographer."

**Tactics:**
- [ ] **Agency reseller plan**: agencies get discounted rate, white-label the output, charge clients at full price. Margin is their incentive, not a referral fee
- [ ] **Shopify Partners Program**: get listed as a recommended tool in the partner ecosystem. Build relationships with 10–20 mid-size agencies that specialize in fashion/apparel Shopify builds
- [ ] **"Photographer to AI" pivot package**: offer creative directors and e-commerce photographers a way to offer "AI-enhanced photoshoots" to their clients at 10x the volume — they become the best salespeople because they're trusted by the ICP

> Defer until 100+ happy customers to point to. Don't sell B2B without proof.

## Priority Order — Next 90 Days

| Priority | Channel | Why Now |
|----------|---------|---------|
| 1 | Output virality | Zero CAC, implement immediately |
| 2 | Competitive displacement | Manual outreach to Botika/SellerPic 1-star reviewers this week. 20 emails → 5–10 trials → best early case studies |
| 3 | TikTok demo | Record one demo video. Don't overthink production. Ship it. |
| 4 | Print-on-demand conversation | Takes months to close — start now |
| 5 | Agency channel | After 100+ happy customers |

---

## The Logic

Order matters. Virality and displacement have near-zero cost and build the case study base. Case studies are what unlock the agency and partnership channels, which are what unlock $10M scale.

**$1M ARR** = ~1,700 customers at $49/month = 0.3% of 520K+ Shopify apparel stores. Achievable with channels 1–3 alone.

**$10M ARR** = ~17,000 customers = channels 4–5 firing + low churn. The churn problem is solved by quality and consistency, not by growth hacks.
